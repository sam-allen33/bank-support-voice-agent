## Account fees
- Monthly maintenance fee: $0 for Everyday Checking. $12/month for Premier Checking (waived if balance stays above $1,500).
- Overdraft fee: $29 per item. Maximum of 3 overdraft fees per day.
- Out-of-network ATM fee: $2.50 per withdrawal, plus any fee the ATM owner charges.
- Wire transfer fee: $25 domestic, $40 international (outgoing). Incoming wires are free.
- Replacement debit card: $0 for standard delivery (5–7 business days), $25 for expedited (1–2 business days).
- Paper statement fee: $3/month. Free if you switch to electronic statements.

## Transfers and payments
- Internal transfers between Northwind accounts are instant.
- ACH transfers to other banks take 1–3 business days.
- A new external account must be linked and verified before sending money, which can take 1–2 business days.

## Cards
- To freeze a lost or stolen card, the cardholder can request it any time; freezing is reversible.
- A frozen card blocks new purchases but still allows recurring bills you have approved.
- A replacement card is mailed once a card is reported lost or stolen.

## Disputes
- You can dispute a transaction within 60 days of the statement date it appears on.
- Provisional credit is typically issued within 10 business days while the dispute is investigated.

## Support hours
- Phone support: 24/7 for lost/stolen cards and fraud.
- General support: 7am–10pm local time, every day.
